# Draft — not legal advice

## Necessary

Necessary cookies stay on. They cover the sign-in session and the workspace mode you picked. The product does not offer a switch to turn these off.

## Analytics

Analytics stays off unless you allow it. The consent cookie records that choice. Joined does not load an analytics vendor while this draft is in place, and a declined or missing choice stays off.

## How to change your choice

Use the cookie banner to allow or decline analytics. A new consent version asks again. Necessary cookies are not part of that choice.
