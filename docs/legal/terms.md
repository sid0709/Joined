# Draft — not legal advice

## Using Joined

Joined is a job search and hiring product. These draft terms cover a signed-in job seeker or hiring account. They are not counsel-approved and they are not legal advice.

## Accounts

Keep your sign-in accurate. You can export or delete your account from settings. Deletion does not erase records we must keep for tax, fraud, or a legal hold.

## Job listings

Listings point at an employer’s official posting. Joined does not promise that a listing is still open, that you will be hired, or that a score describes you.

## Applications

You submit your own applications. Do not impersonate someone else or send information you do not have the right to share.

## Acceptable use

Do not scrape the product in a way that breaks the service, bypass access controls, or use another person’s account.

## Ending the agreement

You may stop using Joined and delete your account. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or that we cannot keep secure.
