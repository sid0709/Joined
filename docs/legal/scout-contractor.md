# Draft — not legal advice

## Independent contractor

Scout work is piece-rate independent-contractor work. Accepting these terms does not make you an employee, partner, or agent of Joined. This draft is not a determination of worker classification, and counsel still has to review it before launch.

## Piece rate

Rewards follow the published rulebook: a share when a qualifying outcome lands on a job you submitted. Volume alone does not pay. Amounts, hold periods, and daily limits come from the live rulebook shown during onboarding, not from this page.

## No tax advice

You are responsible for your own taxes. A W-9 or W-8BEN may be required before a payout. This page does not tell you which form to file and it is not tax advice.

## Your submissions

Submit an official employer or ATS link, in your own words. Do not fabricate a role, proxy an application, or earn on a job where you are also the candidate, client, or bidder.

## Payouts

Payouts stay off until identity and tax checks pass. A screening hold or a missing tax form blocks a transfer. Test-mode providers do not send live funds.
