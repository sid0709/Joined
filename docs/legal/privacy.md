# Draft — not legal advice

## What we store

A signed-in account can include a profile, résumés, applications, interviews, messages, saved searches, and billing status. Scout accounts also store submissions, earnings, and payout details.

## Why we store it

We store that data to run search, applications, hiring tools, scout rewards, and the account you asked for. We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

Export and delete live in settings. Cookie choices are separate from the consent you give when you apply or when you connect a calendar. You can turn analytics off. Necessary cookies stay on so the session works.

## Retention

We keep account data until you delete it, except records required for legal or financial retention such as a ledger, tax form, or fraud review. The internal retention table in the compliance notes is the working list until counsel replaces it.

## Processors

Hosting, payments, email, and identity checks may be handled by processors under their own terms. Payment card data is collected by the payment processor, not stored as a full card number in Joined.
