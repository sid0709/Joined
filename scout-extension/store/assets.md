## Screenshots (required for the listing)

Provide at least one. Chrome accepts 1280×800 or 640×400, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha.

| Slot         | Size     | Subject to capture                                        |
| ------------ | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (required) | 1280×800 | Side panel signed-out, Sign in to Scout visible           |
| 2            | 1280×800 | Side panel signed-in, scout name, email, and level        |
| 3            | 1280×800 | Optional: sign-in tab on Scoutwell next to the open panel |
